Privacy Policy: Asrar Al-Aql Uploader

Last updated: October 26, 2026

Asrar Al-Aql Uploader ("the app") is a private tool used solely by the owner of the "أسرار العقل" (Asrar Al-Aql) YouTube channel to upload and schedule that channel's own videos and to read that channel's own analytics. It is not offered to the public.

Data the app accesses

  • Through Google OAuth, the channel owner grants the following YouTube API scopes: youtube.upload, youtube.readonly and yt-analytics.readonly.
  • The app uses them only to: upload videos to the owner's channel; set titles, descriptions and publish times; read the owner's channel analytics; and list publicly trending videos.

How data is stored and shared

  • The OAuth refresh token is stored as an encrypted environment secret controlled by the channel owner.
  • No personal data of other users is collected.
  • No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.
  • The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
  • Use of YouTube data is also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

The owner can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
